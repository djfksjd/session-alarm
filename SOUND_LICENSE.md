# Sound provenance and license

## Bundled animal-themed sounds

Thirty-nine built-in WAV files are deterministic notification tones generated by
[`generate_builtin_sounds.py`](plugins/session-alarm/scripts/generate_builtin_sounds.py).
Their stable generator seed, SHA-256 checksum, and `project_generated` provenance are recorded in
[`sources.json`](plugins/session-alarm/assets/sounds/sources.json). These generated files are
covered by the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE). They are animal-themed alerts, not recordings
of real animals.

The elephant file is an independently verified external recording:
[“Elephant Trumpeting” by DRAGON-STUDIO](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/nature-elephant-trumpeting-494313/).
It is used under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), subject to the prohibited
uses in Pixabay's [Terms of Service](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/). Its normalized waveform
correlates 0.998711 with the official source download after encoder-delay alignment.

The elephant recording is incorporated as a functional notification sound and is not offered as
standalone stock audio. Do not extract, sell, sublicense, or redistribute it separately. Nothing
in this repository transfers ownership of that recording or grants rights beyond the Pixabay
Content License.

Run `python3 plugins/session-alarm/scripts/generate_builtin_sounds.py --check` to fail closed if a
generated asset or the mixed-license manifest drifts.

## User-imported sounds

The `custom add` command can copy a user's local WAV into their private Session Alarm data
directory. Those files are never uploaded or added to this repository. Their original rights and
license remain unchanged. Users are responsible for importing only audio they created or are
authorized to use.
